# FLOOR → DATA → DECISION → IMPROVE

## FLOOR
Where the work happens.

Retail execution creates the operational activity that eventually becomes measurable.

## DATA
Where the work becomes measurable.

Data can show what changed, where an exception occurred and where investigation may be needed.

## DECISION
Where evidence meets business context.

Ask:
- What happened?
- Why did it happen?
- What evidence supports the explanation?
- What action should follow?

## IMPROVE
Where the decision changes what happens next.

The objective is not simply to report a result. It is to use insight to support better execution and future decisions.

> A number can identify an exception, but it does not always identify the cause.
