# Merchandising Report Case Study

## Scenario
A merchandising report indicated that a product was unavailable at a store.

A physical shelf check showed the product was present.

## Investigation
The reported information was compared with the physical retail environment, including the shelf and product placement.

## Outcome
The information was corrected.

## Analytical process
**REPORT → SHELF CHECK → DISCREPANCY → INVESTIGATION → CORRECTED INFORMATION → BETTER DECISION**

## Learning
A report can identify that something is unusual without explaining why it is unusual.

Operational context can therefore improve the questions asked about business data.

> A number can identify an exception, but it does not always identify the cause.
